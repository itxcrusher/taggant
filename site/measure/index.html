Measure a camera

Point this at the postcard artwork shown at a width you have measured: printed, or on another screen, either works. It reports how much of the world your camera's picture covers, which is the one number the print readiness report assumes rather than measures. Hold it close: the artwork has to fill most of the picture before it can be recognised at all, so start at about 150 mm from a 148 mm wide copy and back off until it is lost.

Hold it square on, and turning sideways matters more than tipping. Tipping shortens one horizontal edge and stretches the other, which cancels in the width this reads; turning shortens both together and nothing cancels. Past about fifteen degrees the reading says TILTED and will not record, because a tilted reading is wrong rather than merely noisy. The figure is measured across the picture's width, so it depends on which way up the phone is: the camera line says which, and a phone held upright gives the narrower of its two fields.
